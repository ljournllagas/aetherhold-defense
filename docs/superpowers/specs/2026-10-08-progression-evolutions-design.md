# Tower evolution and replay progression

Date: 2026-10-08. Tier: Full. Status: approved by the user for implementation planning.

Review: round 1 scored 8/10 with one victory-reward lifecycle blocker; resolved by defining non-activating reward choices at victory and aligning the Run glossary. Round 2 scored 9/10 with zero blockers and zero advisory findings. Gameplay, balance, and runtime verification remain implementation work.

## Purpose and authority

The player reported exhausting tower upgrades around wave 10, leaving repeated Start Wave actions without further development. The current code has five archetypes, four levels each, nine build plots, indefinitely generated waves, and temporary/consumable relics. This design makes wave 10 a progression transition and gives repeat runs new strategic choices.

The user confirmed the scope and defaults below. This feature spec is a proposed amendment to docs/SPEC.md, not a competing permanent baseline. Upon approval, update that baseline in the implementation change for evolution, achievements, victory, endless, and score semantics. Preserve the approved responsive-playability contract and existing original fantasy visual identity. No application changes or deployment are part of writing this document. The workspace has no Git repository; saving the spec is possible, committing it is not.

## Approaches considered

1. **Recommended: explicit tower branches, a small unlock profile, and a siege state machine.** Progression decisions and effective combat stats are independent of Phaser presentation. Combat consumes those stats/effects; scenes render and dispatch actions. This supports ten distinct evolutions without duplicating foundational towers or networking unlocks.
2. Extend each tower's flat levels indefinitely. Simpler initially, but does not express alternative roles or achievement-locked branches; conflicts with the agreed strategic variety.
3. A general talent/loadout/reward framework. Flexible, but adds systems the player did not choose and makes progression harder to explain. Excluded from this release.

## Agreed experience and scope

- A standard siege has 30 waves, bosses at 10, 20, and 30, and a victory decision after clearing wave 30 and killing its scheduled final boss.
- Target 20–30 minutes for a competent Medium run at 1x including ordinary preparation decisions. This is a playtest target, not a hard countdown or mandatory wait.
- Levels 1–4 retain their existing foundation identity. Level 4 becomes “Ready to evolve” rather than a permanent final upgrade.
- Defeating the scheduled wave-10 boss unlocks evolution for that run. A level-4 tower purchases one branch at evolution rank 0, then three further upgrades to ranks 1, 2, and 3. Later towers follow the same path.
- Each archetype has its starter branch from the first run; one alternative unlocks through its branch achievement. No permanent damage, gold, or lives bonuses.
- After victory, players may finish the run or continue into endless with the same defense. Fully evolved towers gain repeatable mastery purchases only in endless.
- Unlocks persist locally in this browser and device. Clearing site data resets them. No accounts, cloud sync, save export, currency shop, new maps, heroes, or new relic system. Saving/resuming an in-progress run is outside this release.

## Tower progression and purchase contract

Represent foundation level (1–4), branch (none or a valid archetype branch), evolution rank (0–3 when evolved), mastery rank (integer >=0), and actual total gold invested explicitly. Do not index existing four-frame art or four-entry stats arrays with evolution/mastery ranks.

Purchases are atomic: recheck run state, selected tower identity/existence, prerequisites, next rank, branch availability, and current gold at commit time. On failure spend nothing, preserve the tower, and show the reason. Repeated input cannot purchase a rank twice from one stale action. Buying a branch is one deliberate action after its name, effect, price, and permanent-for-this-tower commitment are shown; no extra confirmation modal.

Building, foundational upgrades, evolution, and selling retain existing during-wave permissions; evolution is available after the wave-10 boss's death even while its remaining enemies are alive. Pause/modal/background/terminal blockers remain enforced. Branch selection can be canceled without spending. All eligible towers use the run-start snapshot of unlocked branches, so an achievement earned during this run does not change its available branches.

Each tower commits to one branch. Selling and rebuilding permits a different available branch but requires repurchasing levels and evolution. Refund floor(0.70 * actual total spent), including evolution and mastery; never recompute historic investment from subsequently tuned prices. A sale removes owned aura/ground effects immediately; debuffs already on enemies expire normally. Cooldowns, targeting, and position survive upgrades. No upgrade grants an immediate free attack, resets special-attack counters, heals enemies, or duplicates an in-flight impact. Each projectile snapshots its offensive stats on firing; later upgrades/sales do not rewrite that projectile. Aura contribution is sampled at firing.

## Evolution roster and effect semantics

Each row describes initial tuning seeds for a coherent implementation, not a claim that balance is verified. Store all coefficients in typed configuration. The implementation may tune these numbers to meet the acceptance targets, recording final values and playtest evidence; it must preserve the approved branch identities, purchase stages, and unlock requirements.

Common seed progression: take level-4 stats as the reference; raw damage multipliers for evolution ranks 0–3 are [1.20, 1.55, 2.00, 2.60], interval multipliers [1.00, 0.97, 0.94, 0.90], range multipliers [1.00, 1.03, 1.06, 1.10]. Then apply the branch's modifiers. Preserve non-overridden splash/slow/chain stats. Round raw damage to integer before existing armor calculation. These are derived from the reference, never compounded by repeatedly mutating current stats.

| Archetype | Starter branch | Alternative branch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ranger | Marksman: raw damage x1.35, interval x1.25; damage to bosses x1.50. Physical, one target. | Volley: raw damage x0.55 per arrow; at most three distinct enemies in tower range per attack. Physical; no duplicate arrows at one enemy. |
| Bombard | Siegebreaker: original physical splash, treating each victim's physical armor as 50% of its current value. | Flame Mortar: raw damage x0.75 physical impact with original splash, plus an elemental burning field at the impact point. |
| Frost | Winterguard: original elemental slow; every fifth successful primary hit additionally freezes that target for 0.5s (boss: 0.15s). | Brittle Ice: elemental damage; slow factor 0.30 for 2s, plus vulnerability for 3s: incoming damage x[1.15, 1.20, 1.25, 1.30] by evolution rank. |
| Arcane | Spellbreaker: arcane damage treating the victim's current ward armor as 50% of its value. | Arcane Beacon: personal raw damage x0.60; other towers within 160 world units gain raw damage x[1.15, 1.20, 1.25, 1.30] by evolution rank. |
| Tempest | Stormcaller: elemental chain; add [2, 3, 4, 5] targets to the level-4 chain limit. | Thunderlord: raw damage x1.60, chain limit 3; every fifth successful primary hit stuns its primary target for 0.35s (boss: 0.10s). |

Target selection uses the existing targeting mode for the primary. Volley fills remaining targets by that same ordering with stable enemy-ID ties. Chains cannot hit a victim twice per attack; existing chain reach/falloff rules apply. Mark hits successful only if damage lands on a living target; fifth-hit effects count primary impacts, not secondary or ground hits. Counter starts at zero when the tower evolves.

Burning fields: radius equals that shell's splash radius, duration 3s, ticks every 0.5s beginning 0.5s after impact, each tick raw elemental damage equal to 0.30 of the shell's raw impact damage before armor/vulnerability. One active field per tower; a new impact replaces that tower's field. Overlapping fields from different towers deal damage independently. Fields expire on game time and must be gone before wave-clear detection. Each victim takes at most one tick per field per scheduled tick. Catch up scheduled ticks safely on large deltas without damaging an already dead victim or crediting its kill twice.

Beacon affects other towers only, including other Beacons' personal attacks; no self-buff and no recursive aura magnification. Only the largest active Beacon bonus applies to each recipient. Reevaluate membership at fire time. Brittle vulnerability uses the largest unexpired magnitude, with each application tracked until its own expiry; lower new applications cannot extend a stronger application's duration. Vulnerability applies once after armor to tower, ground, and relic damage. Beacon buffs tower/owned-ground raw damage through the shot snapshot, never Meteor. Existing surge/overcharge multipliers apply once to all tower-generated damage; no second multiplication on ground ticks.

Freeze and stun stop movement but not regeneration or boss ability clocks, matching Time Lock's movement behavior. Slow uses the strongest active slow; movement stops while any active freeze/stun applies. Different controls do not add durations. Tower-applied freeze/stun is limited to one application per enemy per 1.5 game seconds, shared across towers; Time Lock remains independently usable. Boss-specific durations apply by enemy isBoss flag. Dead/escaped enemies ignore later effects. Armor penetration never affects the other armor channel or produces negative armor. All durations and attack behavior respect pause, background pause, and 1x/2x/3x.

Evolution effects require readable original indicators: branch name and rank in the inspector; a branch-specific weapon/crown accent on the existing tower; distinguishable arrows, burning area, control/debuff marker, and Beacon radius on selection. A new full sprite atlas is unnecessary. Labels and silhouettes, not color alone, communicate branches.

## Economy, pacing, and endless mastery

Evolution price seeds, identical across branches within an archetype: ceil(level-4 upgrade cost * [1.50, 2.00, 2.75, 3.75]) for purchasing rank 0, then 1, 2, and 3. Integer gold only. Keep early rewards/foundation costs initially; tune late wave composition, HP, rewards, and these coefficients using the gates below. No mandatory inter-wave timer or purchases gated at waves 15/20.

Mastery requires endless, evolution rank 3, and an active playable run. Each rank adds 5% of that branch's rank-3 raw damage, additive: baseRank3Damage * (1 + 0.05 * masteryRank). It improves direct, splash, chain, and owned-ground damage, not aura strength, penetration, vulnerability, control strength/duration, range, or attack rate. Seed cost for next rank m+1: ceil(rank3PurchaseCost * 1.25^(m+1)), where current m starts at 0. Display rank, next damage, and cost. Numerically unsafe/unrepresentable next prices or stats disable purchasing with a visible limit reason; never permit NaN, infinity, rounding to a free upgrade, or loss of integer currency precision. There is no ordinary gameplay rank cap.

Mastery extends meaningful spending, not guaranteed indefinite survival. Endless enemy pressure continues to increase using varied composition and bosses. No mandatory additional final victory. Rank-3 siege upgrades remain buyable in endless for unfinished towers; mastery follows completion. All run development resets on restart; only alternative branches remain unlocked.

## Branch achievements and local persistence

Exactly five achievements, one per archetype. On successful completion of wave 20, inspect towers still present. For each archetype with at least one starter-branch tower at evolution rank >=2, unlock its alternative. Any difficulty qualifies; multiple achievements may unlock together. Purchasing after wave 20 does not retroactively qualify; a later run can try again. Selling the qualifying tower before the wave-clear event prevents qualification. All scheduled enemies may be killed or leak as permitted by lives; defeating wave 20's boss is not an additional achievement requirement. A run that reaches zero lives does not complete the wave or earn that event's achievements.

Evaluate once from the authoritative wave-complete event, after combat consequences and before the next-wave/victory UI. Keep earned unlocks after defeat, quit, restart, or victory. Save at earn time, not only game over. Alternatives become available starting with the next run because availability is snapshotted at run creation. Never unlock from QA grants, previews, render/resize callbacks, or debug-assisted runs. Display the requirement and current run qualification state in a menu Progression panel and on a locked branch; show earned achievements in a brief nonblocking notice and the results summary.

Store a separate versioned unlock profile; preserve current settings keys and personal-best data. Only recognized branch IDs qualify. Empty profile means all starter branches, no alternatives. Read failures or malformed known-version data fall back to a usable starter profile with an understandable progress-storage warning. Do not overwrite unreadable or newer unsupported-version data automatically. Writes merge recognized persisted unlocks with current earned unlocks, are idempotent, and preserve the original earned timestamp for each branch. Two tabs normally converge by listening to storage events and merging before writes; the next run reloads the persisted union. This browser-local collection is not an authoritative anti-cheat system.

On write failure keep the unlock in memory, display “Unlock earned, but progress could not be saved”, and retry on the next earn and next run creation. Clearly distinguish session-available unlocks from confirmed saved unlocks. A failed backend request never removes unlocks. Removing/resetting unlocks from the UI is excluded; browser site-data clearing still resets the profile. Existing local personal bests are retained as legacy records when the score era changes, and new best comparisons use the current score version rather than a legacy incompatible score.

## Siege state and final boss

Run phases: siege preparation/active, victory decision, endless preparation/active, and terminal result. User/modal/background pause remain orthogonal blockers. Wave 30 explicitly uses one scheduled Warlord as the final boss with its existing enrage and summoning abilities; late-wave HP/composition must be tuned against evolved defenses. Do not silently use the current post-20 multi-boss escalation as the final siege definition. Final-boss warnings and preview label wave 30 as the siege finale.

Evolution opens only on killing the scheduled wave-10 boss, not a spawned bonus target. If that boss escapes while lives remain, end the run with a clear “Siege failed: the first boss escaped” result: it cannot otherwise continue through a siege without the agreed progression. If the final scheduled wave-30 boss escapes, similarly end with “Siege failed: the final boss escaped”. These failure paths are explicit safeguards for the two required boss milestones. Other leaks, including the wave-20 boss, follow existing lives rules.

Victory requires wave 30 fully resolved (spawn queue, living enemies, flights, active ground effects empty), final boss killed, and lives >0. Boss death alone is insufficient. Apply the wave-clear bonus and achievements exactly once. Zero lives takes precedence over victory. Cancel any uncommitted Meteor target through the existing vault cancellation path without consuming its reserved relic, then enter victory decision and freeze simulation and battlefield/build/upgrade/relic-activation input. Victory/reward navigation input remains available. Show “Siege complete” with score/lives/unlocks, and offer Finish Run and Continue Endless after pending rewards are resolved. Preserve this decision, rewards, branch state, and pause reasons on resize/backgrounding.

At victory decision, resolve pending relics in FIFO order using Store when space exists, Replace Oldest when full, or Discard New. Relic activation and Use Oldest are unavailable in this phase, with the reason “Continue into endless to use relics”; never auto-use a relic. This explicitly prevents Meteor targeting, repair changing the frozen result, or a Treasure Creature spawning on a cleared battlefield. A full inventory always has Replace/Discard exits. Stored relics carry into endless; Finish ends the run with remaining stored relics unused. This temporary restriction applies only at victory decision; earlier wave/reward behavior stays unchanged. Reward reveal and navigation use visible UI time while simulation remains frozen; backgrounding suspends them and preserves pending selections. Finish/Continue are disabled until the vault has no pending reward or target and no reward modal is open; an empty queue needs no extra interaction. Repeated resolution cannot store or replace twice. No enemy spawns or gameplay effects can occur in victory decision.

Continue resumes the same run ID, towers, gold, lives, targeting, relic inventory, and cumulative statistics; next wave is 31 and mastery opens. No victory score is submitted at this checkpoint. Finish creates one terminal victory result and exposes the normal manual score-submission flow. Defeat in endless creates one terminal result with the cumulative score and a visible prior siege victory. Restart/quit perform existing cleanup, including fields, aura/effect subscriptions, hit counters, transient notifications, and modal/input callbacks. Repeated clicks cannot end/continue twice, start wave 31 before choosing Continue, or submit two result snapshots for one run.

## Score and backend compatibility

Bump GAME_VERSION and SCORE_VERSION for the new gameplay era; retain stored old-era scores and filter current rankings as today. Keep ordering by highest wave, score, and earliest timestamp. No bonus for owning unlocks.

Add explicit wavesCompleted and outcome (victory, defeat, siege-failed) to the terminal score contract. highestWave is the highest entered wave; a victory ending at wave 30 has highestWave=wavesCompleted=30. Defeat or siege-failed during wave w has highestWave=w and wavesCompleted=w-1. Any pre-first-wave result uses highestWave=1 and wavesCompleted=0 for compatibility with minimum-wave validation. An endless defeat follows the same incomplete-current-wave rule. Final result remainingLives is actual lives, not forcibly zero for positive-lives siege failure. Derive wave bonuses, spawn/duration plausibility, kill bounds, repairs, and rewards from explicit completion and outcome; no blanket assumption that highestWave-1 waves always completed.

The Worker rejects invalid completion/outcome combinations, a victory before/after exactly completed wave 30, victory without the required wave-10 and wave-30 bosses killed, non-finite fields, incompatible score versions, and duplicate run IDs. Because the agreed achievement/victory flow permits the wave-20 boss to escape, victory does not require all three bosses killed; represent a siegeBossesDefeated milestone mask in the payload for these named requirements and validate that it is consistent with bossesKilled and entered/completed waves. This mask is a client claim checked for plausibility, not authoritative replay verification. Add new fields through an additive D1 migration with legacy defaults; never delete old scores. Current client and Worker must share the new payload and validation. API unavailability leaves victory/endless playable and keeps local results/unlocks.

Always show current role/rank, next stats, cost, and a specific disabled reason: reach level 4, defeat wave-10 boss, complete branch achievement, insufficient gold, finish evolution, continue into endless, or paused/ended. A locked alternative remains inspectable but not purchasable. The menu Progression panel shows five achievements and both branches per archetype without taking permanent battlefield space. Use existing tokens, fonts, sheet scrolling, 44px touch targets, safe-area layout, focus affordances, and reduced-motion rules. Victory/result actions remain reachable in both orientations.

Suggested cohesive boundaries (the Full plan determines exact files): typed evolution configuration/effective stats; pure purchase eligibility and investment accounting; effect/status resolution consumed by combat; branch-achievement evaluator; local unlock repository; siege lifecycle transition rules; shared result/scoring validation. Phaser scenes own presentation and dispatch, not storage validation, branch arithmetic, or duplicated victory detection. Reuse existing economy, reward vault, projectile, pause, and wave systems. No new framework or product dependency is required.
