# Visual review

## Evolution masters — inspected individually

All fourteen were opened at full source resolution: Marksman, Volley, Siegebreaker, Flame Mortar, Winterguard, Brittle Ice, Spellbreaker, Arcane Beacon, Stormcaller, Thunderlord, Deadeye, Headhunter, Pyre and Contagion.

Observed: one coherent tower per file, differentiated weapon/crystal/furnace silhouettes, consistent painterly fantasy construction and elevated perspective, no UI text or accidental multi-frame grids. Solid structures, weapon tips and bases are visible without obvious clipping. All are static branch appearances, not foundation progression sheets. Small heraldic motifs are painted decorative emblems, not control labels.

Limits: exact orthographic angle and requested whitespace are not mechanically guaranteed. Several masters fill nearly the canvas height and contain faint alpha residue outside their solid silhouette. Keep raw source files and use recorded alpha diagnostics when deriving display bounds. This review does not establish a precise muzzle anchor, small-screen readability, road clearance or runtime compatibility. Integration still needs scaled rendering and contact checks.

## Reference review

All ten current boss atlases were opened to establish existing identities before portrait generation. The Classic ancient-border-keep-map-v3.webp was opened before requesting lighting-only variants. References are identity/geometry constraints, not evidence that the resulting files preserve them; inspect each output separately.

## World sigils — inspected individually

All ten were opened: Borderkeep, Emberfall, Frostveil, Whisperfen, Sunken Aether, Ironroot, Sky Isles, Dreadmoon, Astral Rift and Aether Crown. Each is a single sculpted ceremonial artifact with a distinct world motif, no baked text and no accidental grid. The foliage seal, molten core, ice crown, hydra heart, oracle eye, foundry cog, storm fang, eclipsed moon, rift knot and fractured crown match their intended use cases. Their detailed ornament will need scaled UI review; raw alpha-edge diagnostics remain in the file report.

## Finale and Last Lantern — inspected individually

The finale is a coherent wide illustration with central restored keep/beacon, distant campaign biomes and ten foreground trophies. No baked UI text was observed. It is intended as result-screen illustration, not a battlefield.

Night and dawn plates visually retain the original Classic road loops, bridge, keep, waterfall/cliffs and broad clearings while changing lighting. They require exact runtime route/plot/beacon overlay comparison before production; this source inspection is not pixel-perfect registration proof.

## Amber Hourglass — inspected individually

The vista establishes bronze hourglasses, sandstone ruins, amber storms and teal shadows. The terrain has a broad open center and perimeter landmarks without painted routes or plot circles. Chronolens, Clockmite, Pendulum Knight, Hourkeeper and Unwound King each show one coherent complete static design, distinct silhouettes and consistent materials. No accidental animation grids or UI text were observed. The alpha-at-16 boundary check found no solid cutout touching an edge, including the King's tall staff/crown. This kit is preproduction: routes, balancing and creature animation are not implemented or verified.

## Boss portraits and repairs

All ten original portrait outputs were opened and compared in broad identity against the existing atlases: moss/bark/violet Warden; molten stone Colossus; ice-crowned Matriarch and staff; mossy multiheaded Hydra; turquoise/bronze masked Oracle; furnace-armored Tyrant; blue/gold storm beast; spectral purple Regent; armored portal-bearing Sovereign; ivory/gold/violet Devourer. These are expressive painted interpretations of existing sprites, not pixel-identical enlargements or replacement combat atlases.

Warden, Oracle, Regent and Sovereign originals had upper framing defects. All four v2 repairs were individually opened and inspected: antler/crown/upper shoulder tips now fit against additional biome background, with identity retained. These v2 files are the selected masters; originals are retained under drafts/. repair-queue.json records all four as resolved. No portrait crop repair remains pending.
